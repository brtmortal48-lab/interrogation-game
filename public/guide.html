OFFICIAL GAME GUIDE

🎭 How to Play The Interrogation

A live social deduction mystery game for streamers, players, and viewers.

Back Home

⚡ Quick Start

1

Streamer creates a room

The streamer opens the Streamer Dashboard, joins as detective, and copies the player invite link.

2

Players join

Players enter a display name or load a Profile ID to keep points.

3

Start the case

Every player receives a role, alibi, motive, relationship, evidence fragment, objective, and ability.

4

Interrogate

The streamer questions players, compares alibis, uses Stream Events, and watches the Detective Director.

5

Vote and accuse

Viewers and players can vote. The streamer makes the final accusation.

6

Reveal the truth

The case ending reveals the murderer, points, bonus objectives, and profile progress.

👤 Player Guide

Your goal

Talk, defend your alibi, compare stories, reveal useful information, and survive suspicion. If you are the Murderer or Accomplice, mislead the room without exposing yourself.

Public Alibi

Your public alibi is the story everyone can question. Be ready to explain where you were, what you were doing, and who can verify it.

Private Evidence

Your evidence fragment is only part of the truth. Do not assume it solves the case alone.

Relationship

You may trust, suspect, argue with, or have a connection to another player. Use this to create discussion.

Motive

Even innocent players can have suspicious motives. Having a motive does not mean you are guilty.

Abilities

Use your role ability carefully. Some abilities protect, some reveal, some confuse, and some mislead.

Important: The best way to play is to keep talking. Ask others questions, challenge weak alibis, and explain your own story clearly.

🎥 Streamer / Detective Guide

Start with alibis

Ask every player: “Where were you?”, “What were you doing?”, and “Who can confirm it?”

Use the Detective Director

The Director gives live suggestions based on votes, contradictions, suspicion, and case intensity.

Use Stream Events

Force Alibis, Random Spotlight, Emergency Vote, and New Lead are pacing tools. Use them when the round slows down.

Do not trust evidence blindly

Evidence fragments are incomplete. Some may be corrupted or manipulated.

Watch contradictions

If a player changes location or story, pressure them. Contradictions are conversation fuel.

Final accusation

Use alibis, motives, viewer votes, suspicion, and behavior before making the final call.

Good detective questions

  • Why were you near that area?
  • Who can confirm your story?
  • Why do you trust that player?
  • Your motive looks suspicious. Explain it.
  • Your current statement conflicts with your earlier alibi. Which one is true?

🗳 Viewer Guide

Viewers do not need a Twitch login or account. They can open the Viewer Vote link and vote for who they think the murderer is.

Vote anytime

You can change your vote before the reveal.

Watch the overlay

The OBS overlay shows suspects, votes, contradictions, stream events, and case intensity.

Help the streamer

Chat can suggest questions, point out contradictions, and push the detective toward suspicious players.

🎭 Roles

😈 Murderer

Hide your real truth, survive accusation, and use sabotage tools to mislead the investigation.

🕶 Accomplice

Protect the murderer without looking coordinated.

👁 Witness

You usually have stronger information, but it is still not the full truth.

🧍 Observer

Question alibis and watch for contradictions.

🚶 Drifter

Your movement may look suspicious. Explain yourself clearly.

🛡 Guard

Protect a player and reduce suspicion.

📰 Journalist

Publish reports that create pressure.

🕵 Informant

Receive private hints and guide questions carefully.

⚖ Lawyer

Defend a player and reduce suspicion.

📊 Analyst

Use system-style analysis to help compare timelines.

🧩 Game Systems

Profiles

Players and hosts can create Profile IDs to keep points, ranks, stats, and progress.

Bonus Objectives

Each role may have a secret bonus goal. Completing it earns extra progress.

Evidence Fragments

Evidence is intentionally incomplete. Combine fragments through discussion.

Murderer Sabotage

The murderer can use tools like false leads, framing, corrupted logs, and blurred evidence.

Case Intensity

The round gets more intense when suspicion rises, votes spike, contradictions appear, or events trigger.

Feedback Center

Players and streamers can submit feedback, bug reports, and feature ideas to help improve the game.
