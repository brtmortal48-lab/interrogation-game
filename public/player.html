Player Guide

What to do

Keep talking. Defend your public alibi, compare stories, use your ability, and expose contradictions.

Roles

  • Murderer: Hide your real location and plant a false lead.
  • Accomplice: Help the murderer survive without looking coordinated.
  • Witness: Push a stronger observation.
  • Observer: Watch contradictions and use anonymous notes.
  • Drifter: Your messy movement can confuse the timeline.
  • Guard: Protect a player’s alibi and reduce suspicion.
  • Journalist: Publish an anonymous report.
  • Informant: Receive a private hint.
  • Lawyer: Defend someone and reduce suspicion.
  • Analyst: Publish system-style analysis.
