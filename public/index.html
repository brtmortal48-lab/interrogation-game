How to Play

Streamer

You are the detective. Read evidence, question players, build a theory, pressure suspects, then accuse one player before revealing the truth.

Players

Each player gets a secret role and private observation. Your clue may be true, incomplete, or misleading.

Goal

The culprit tries to escape. Witnesses try to help. Misinformed players may accidentally mislead. Observers survive by reading the room.
