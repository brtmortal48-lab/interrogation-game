QUICK GUIDE

How to Play The Interrogation

Stay on this page while learning the rules. Pick the section you need.

⚡ 30 Second Version

1Streamer creates a room and shares player link.
2Players join, get role, alibi, motive, relationship, and evidence.
3Streamer questions alibis and uses the Detective Director.
4Players talk, defend, accuse, vote, and reveal clues carefully.
5Streamer accuses one person and reveals the case.

👤 Player Basics

  • Alibi: your public story. Defend it when questioned.
  • Evidence Fragment: only part of the truth. Do not assume it proves everything.
  • Motive: even innocent players may look suspicious.
  • Relationship: use it to explain who you trust, argued with, or observed.
  • Ability: use it once at the right moment to help, mislead, or protect.

🎥 Streamer Flow

  • Start in Overview and read the case file.
  • Open Players and ask one person at a time about their alibi.
  • Use Detective Director suggestions when you are unsure what to ask.
  • Use Events when the room slows down: Force Alibis, Spotlight, Vote, New Lead.
  • Use Board to organize Trusted, Persons of Interest, Major Suspects, and Prime Suspect.

🗳 Viewer Role

  • Open the vote link and vote for who you think is the murderer.
  • You can change your vote before reveal.
  • Viewer votes influence the streamer and appear on the OBS overlay.

🧠 Game Systems

  • Contradictions: if players change locations, the game flags it.
  • Sabotage: the murderer can frame, corrupt, blur, or plant false leads.
  • Intensity: rises when suspicion, votes, contradictions, and events increase.
  • Profiles: use Profile ID / Detective ID to keep progress.
